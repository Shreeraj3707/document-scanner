# 📄 Document Scanner

A classical computer vision project that detects, flattens, and enhances documents from photos — like a real scanner app, built from scratch with OpenCV.

## ✨ Features

- **Automatic document detection** — finds the 4 corners of any page, receipt, or notebook
- **Perspective correction** — flattens angled photos into top-down scans
- **Two enhancement modes:** `bw` (black & white) and `color` (shadow removal)
- **Interactive web demo** — drag-and-drop UI built with Gradio
- **Batch processing** — scan multiple documents at once
- **CPU-only** — no GPU required
